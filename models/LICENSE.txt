SF-X500 mesh geometry
====================

The file sf-x500.glb is derived from the x500_base and gimbal models in
PX4-gazebo-models:

  https://github.com/PX4/PX4-gazebo-models

Those upstream files are BSD-3-Clause. Two copyright notices apply. The
notices below are reproduced for the binary redistribution.

Changes made for this lab: part nodes renamed to the lab ids; embedded
cameras removed; flat-shaded vertices welded; triangle count reduced with
meshoptimizer (about 300k to about 58k triangles); normals rebuilt at
runtime. The battery pack in the source conversion was already a simple
placeholder box, not an upstream mesh.
